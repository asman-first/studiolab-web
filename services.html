Questions

Before you write to us.

How do payments work?
50% to start, 50% on delivery. Subscriptions are billed monthly with a 3-month term. Prices are in AED and exclude 5% VAT. We invoice from Studiolab Technologies LLC, Dubai.
How does the consultation work?
It is free: a 30-minute call or a 60-minute visit to our studio in Business Bay. We look at your current process, agree the scope and the timeline, and confirm the price. You get a written summary and a one-page proposal the same day.
Which languages do you support?
Agents and websites ship in Arabic, English and Russian by default, with more on request. Dubbing covers 150+ languages. Arabic is always checked by a native speaker on Pro and Premium.
Who owns the avatar and the content?
You do. We sign a consent and usage agreement for your likeness and voice, and we never reuse your avatar, data or footage for anyone else.
What about our data?
Client data stays in your accounts wherever possible. We work under NDA, use encryption and access controls, and design for GDPR, DIFC and UAE data protection rules.
Can we start small?
Yes. Most clients start with one AI sales agent, one video pack or one automated process, then expand once the numbers are in.
